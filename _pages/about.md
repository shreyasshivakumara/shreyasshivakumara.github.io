I am a PhD student in the Department of Science and Technology at Linköping University (Campus Norrköping), supervised by [Karljohan Lundin Palmerius](https://liu.se/en/employee/karlu20) and co-supervised by [Gabriel Eilertsen](https://liu.se/en/employee/gabei62). Before this, I was a research engineer at CMIV, Linköping University, working with Joel Hedlund and Claes Lundström.

My research is on the **perceptual quality of neural scene representations** — NeRFs and 3D Gaussian Splatting — and how they hold up in **immersive VR**. I study where standard image-quality metrics disagree with what people actually see, and develop evaluation methods that close that gap, including stereoscopic user studies in head-mounted displays.
